SpannBridge connects the Chatbot in COMSOL Multiphysics® software to Claude Code or OpenAI Codex

SpannBridge is free, open-source software for Windows. It lets the Chatbot window in COMSOL Multiphysics® 6.4 use the Claude or ChatGPT subscription that you already have, so you need no API key.

What I do

AI Agents for COMSOL Multiphysics®

I use AI coding agents such as Claude Code and OpenAI Codex to get more out of your COMSOL Multiphysics® models. The aim is to solve real engineering problems, not to use AI for its own sake.

Medical Devices & Pharma

Simulation support for blood-contacting devices, drug delivery, and tissue engineering. My published research covers blood damage and microfluidics.

Clean Energy

Modeling of flow battery cells and stacks, fusion-target physics, and experimental solar concepts. I help teams develop new energy technologies faster.

Nanotechnology

Modeling support for nanomanufacturing, roll-to-roll nanoimprint lithography, and nanoscale transport. I help teams scale lab processes up to production.
