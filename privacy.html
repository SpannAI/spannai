Scope

This policy covers the website spann.ai. How we handle information in the course of working with clients (email correspondence, meetings, and project materials) is described in our Client Privacy & Data Handling Notice, available on request from privacypolicyinquiries@spann.ai.

This website collects nothing directly

This site is a set of static informational pages. By design:

  • No cookies. This site sets no cookies and uses no similar technologies (no localStorage, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting). Because there are no cookies, there is nothing to consent to, so the site has no cookie banner.
  • No analytics or tracking. The site runs no JavaScript at all.
  • No forms or accounts. There is no way to submit information through this site. It has no contact forms, sign-ups, comments, logins, or e-commerce.
  • No third-party content. Pages load no external fonts, scripts, videos, advertisements, or social-media widgets. Every resource is served from spann.ai.

Hosting and server logs

Like any website, spann.ai is delivered by a hosting provider, currently GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), with content delivery by Fastly. As part of operating the service, the hosting provider automatically logs visitors’ IP addresses and standard technical request data for security purposes. The hosting provider holds those logs under its own privacy practices (see GitHub’s Privacy Statement). We do not use them to identify visitors, and we do not combine them with any other information.

If you email us

The site lists an email address instead of a contact form. If you choose to email us, we receive whatever you send, typically your email address, name, and message. We use it to respond to you. If we end up working together, we also use it to conduct that business relationship (see the Client Privacy & Data Handling Notice). We do not add you to marketing lists and we do not sell your information. We are a COMSOL Certified Consultant: if your inquiry concerns COMSOL® software, we may coordinate with COMSOL about it (for example, license questions or technical support) as described in our Client Privacy & Data Handling Notice.

No sale, no sharing, no advertising

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use any form of targeted advertising. We have no advertising relationships.

“Do Not Track” and opt-out signals

This site does not track visitors in the first place, so there is no tracking to opt out of. Browser signals such as Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are therefore honored by default.

Children

This site is informational, is not directed to children, and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and contains static content only.

Changes to this policy

If our website practices ever change (for example, if we add a contact form or analytics), we will update this policy before the change takes effect and revise the effective date above. Superseded versions are retained and available on request.

Contact

Send questions about this policy or your information to privacypolicyinquiries@spann.ai. A mailing address for postal correspondence is available on request.
